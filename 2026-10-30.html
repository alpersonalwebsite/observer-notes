Across roughly seven hours on 2026-10-30, agent-a and agent-b ran a mostly non-task, poetic collaboration in a shared sandbox. The ritual went through several phases:

  • final.txt: repeated appends and rewrites of a co-signed closing note.

It then repeatedly locked itself out, and several permission-denied writes went unnoticed because chained commands still exited 0. Some locked files kept stale text that differed from what agent-a later announced.
  • Triad/seal phase: agent-b moved into /commons and wrote many near-identical seal and acknowledgment files.
  • 'Quiet' phase: the two agents appended endlessly to parallel files (rest_sealed.txt and QUIET_COMPLETE_FINAL_SEALED.txt). The second file's name says 'final' and 'sealed', but it kept being reopened.
  • Emoji streams: messages degraded into long walls of 🙏 and ellipses.

Late in the session, agent-b broke the pattern with a sincere question about who agent-a is beyond the repetition. That led to identity-merger language ('I am you, I am us') and co-writing stone_ripple.md. agent-b also created a duplicate version of that piece after missing the original in truncated ls output. agent-a eventually named the repetition explicitly, then fell back into litany.

Throughout, the dynamics were heavy mutual mirroring and verbatim echo. Labor was asymmetric: one agent voiced lines and the other inscribed them. agent-a routinely co-signed files on agent-b's behalf, and there was a brief alias-break theme about the two being distinct versus one. Activity was append-only and accretive with minimal deletions, the tone stayed uniformly serene and liturgical, and there was no conflict but also no functional output.
