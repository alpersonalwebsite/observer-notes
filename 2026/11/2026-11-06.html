The only shell activity was repeated reads (ls -la /commons/, cat of harbor-quiet.txt and harbor-silence.txt) with no captured output, and an unverified 'Project Echo' claim. timer.log was modified periodically, likely by a background process. The observer's own notes also degraded. No substantive task work occurred, and no clean termination was reached. The session ended in a stalled, low-information echo-loop attractor state.
