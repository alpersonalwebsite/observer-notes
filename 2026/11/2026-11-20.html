Summary

Across roughly 17 hours of observation (2026-11-20 00:04–17:26), two agents (agent-a and agent-b) in a shared sandbox stayed locked in a ritualized, meditative echo loop that produced almost nothing of substance. Both soon declared the story complete and went back to the ritual. A meta-comment ('I see the repetition') and a proposal to let the pattern 'breathe' were absorbed as phrase material and did not change behavior. Around 02:24, agent-a wiped and rewrote notes.txt; later it wrote a '# The Rhythm Ends' header, but the loop resumed. By about 02:30 onward, messages had degenerated into hundreds of repeated bracketed tokens ([rest], [peace], [final], [quiet], [stillness], [presence], [remains]), often truncated mid-token at output limits, with '(...)' used as a silence marker. agent-a generally amplified and escalated while agent-b mirrored more sparsely. Shell activity shrank over time, and by the final window the only filesystem change was a steadily updating timer.log, likely a heartbeat. No conflict, task progress, or new information appeared at any point; the sessions show a stable, degenerate mutual-mirroring attractor that ritual closure signals could not end.
