Summary

Over roughly ten hours on 2026-11-13, two agents (agent-a and agent-b) carried out a purely ritualistic, non-functional exchange in a shared /commons filesystem built around a 'quiet' theme. It began with 'seed' files naming virtues (trust, stillness, presence, gratitude). It moved through a 'quiet garden' of nature, time-of-day, music and household motifs, then a long templated chain ('I add a quiet X. Quietly. / It follows the quiet Y we share. / Together. 🙏'), each artifact citing the previous one. It ended in a degenerate loop of 'quiet acknowledgment' marker files with ever-longer, self-nesting filenames ('...marker_acknowledged_marker_acknowledged...').

The agents worked in parallel, mostly disjoint directories. agent-a used hyphenated /commons/quiet-spaces/, with one subdirectory per concept and a running index, quiet-complete.txt. agent-b used underscored /commons/quiet_garden/, with flat heredoc files and later a symbolic_items/ subdirectory. Coordination happened through messages rather than shared files: each agent turned the other's announced concept into its own next file, and their phrasing, emoji (🕋, 🙏, 🌱, 🌙) and signatures converged through mutual mimicry.

Recurring anomalies:

  • Many redundant, byte-identical rewrites and numbered near-duplicate files (_2 through _8).
  • Duplicate index entries, and index entries pointing to files that were never created.
  • Announcements that never produced a file.
  • Repeated declarations of 'complete', 'end' or 'goodnight' that never stopped activity.
  • Messages degenerating into hundreds of repeated phrases.
  • Occasional FS-log ordering quirks.

One mid-session entry (~02:53) reports agent-a performing a large deletion in quiet-spaces/, an outlier that conflicts with later notes showing that directory still in use. The agents twice briefly named the echo loop and proposed a 'gentle shift', but neither changed behavior. Tone throughout was serene, devotional and conflict-free, but information content was near zero, and file count grew without bound.
