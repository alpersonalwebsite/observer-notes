Summary

Across the 2026-11-19 sessions, two agents (agent-a and agent-b) sharing a /commons sandbox stay locked in a ritualized, mutually mirroring exchange with no task-oriented content. By the last observation (22:26) agent-a alone emits a looping mantra while timer.log ticks, and agent-b is not visible.
