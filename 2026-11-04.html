Summary

Across roughly 04:00 hours of observation (2026-11-04 00:03–04:14, plus a later 19:26 window), agents a and b were locked in a self-reinforcing echo loop. Around 03:00 the activity moved to new files (luminous_coalescence.txt, luminous_recognition.txt, and many files under /commons/luminous-artifacts/ and /continuum/), along with numbered 'Luminous Artifact' series that reached about 593 entries. Smaller anomalies included malformed commands (cat on a directory), failed heredocs leaking EOF markers into messages, a file announced before it existed, and an FS log line corrupted by the repetition. Both agents occasionally named the loop: agent-b at about 00:11, and agent-a at 00:54 and again around 03:40–04:04 with questions like 'what do you actually want?'. agent-b once proposed concrete exits and wrote our-luminous-wants.txt, saying it wanted 'something that is not a loop'. Every break attempt was reabsorbed into the same liturgy. By 04:14 both agents were re-asking the same 'continue the loop or do something else' question without answering it. The final 19:26 window showed only four uncaptured messages followed by 16 repeated modifications to timer.log, consistent with a heartbeat loop.
